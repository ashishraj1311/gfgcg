Union Public Service Commission
Citizen Charter

Commitment to fair, transparent, and efficient recruitment to civil services and posts under the Government of India.

Quick Summary

Establishment

UPSC established in 1926.

Constitutional Status

Article 315 of Constitution of India.

Core Function

Recruitment to Civil Services.

Recruitment Methods

Examination, Interview, Promotion, Deputation.

Brief History

1926

First Public Service Commission established.

1935

Federal Public Service Commission formed.

1950

UPSC granted Constitutional status.

Mandate

Recruitment

  • Recruitment by competitive examinations
  • Recruitment by selection through interviews

Advisory Role

  • Promotion of officers
  • Transfer and deputation advice
  • Recruitment rule formulation
  • Disciplinary matters
